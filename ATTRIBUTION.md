# Asset provenance and licences

The dark glass materials, nine-slice control textures, background gradient, vector icons, original app icon, display compositions and animation code were authored for Salvage Stack. No LEGO/Tetris branding, studs, board artwork or third-party promotional text was used. No concept mockup is used as a flattened screen background.

**Salvage Grid** is the original 5×7 bitmap font used for the wordmark and short celebratory headings. Its atlas and BMFont definition are in `assets/fonts/`. It is dedicated under CC0 1.0; the licence is included in `assets/fonts/LICENSE.txt`. No attribution is required for this original font.

Instructions, menus and numbers use Godot's built-in fallback sans serif. Godot and its bundled components retain their original licences and notices; include the engine's required notices in any store distribution. See https://godotengine.org/license/ and https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/about/complying_with_licenses.html.

The existing music and effects remain the original synthesized assets created for this game. No commercial samples or Parcel Pilot assets/configuration were introduced.

No external font downloads, native plugins or rendering dependencies were installed for this redesign.
